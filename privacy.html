Privacy Policy

App: Yaad  |  Developer: Umair Jailani  |  Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Yaad collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information. By using Yaad, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: Your name, username, and email address when you register.
  • List and task data: Items, categories, notes, prices, and recurring settings you create inside the app.
  • Location data: Approximate GPS location, only if you enable geo reminders. Used solely to trigger notifications when you are near a saved shop. Not stored beyond what is needed for that feature.
  • Device data: Basic device information used by Firebase and Expo for authentication and push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To sync your lists and items across devices and with group members in real time.
  • To send reminders and notifications you explicitly set up.
  • To enable geo-based shop reminders (location only, when opted in).

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Third Parties

Yaad uses Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) to store your data securely in the cloud. Firebase is operated by Google LLC. Data is stored in the asia-south1 region. Firebase's own privacy policy applies: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Push notifications are delivered via Expo notification infrastructure. Expo's privacy policy: expo.dev/privacy.

4. Data Sharing

Your list data is shared with members of groups you create or join inside the app. This sharing is intentional and initiated by you. No other sharing occurs.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab inside the app. Deletion removes your account and all associated data from Firestore.

6. Children's Privacy

Yaad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Firebase with access rules that restrict data to authenticated users only. Passwords are never stored — Firebase handles authentication securely.

8. Your Rights

  • Access or correct your data via the Profile tab.
  • Delete your account and all data via the Profile tab.
  • Opt out of notifications via the Preferences section in the app.
  • Opt out of geo reminders by disabling location access in your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
